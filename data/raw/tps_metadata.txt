tps.csv is an alteration of the transporters.csv file. This retains information on the 12 main genes of interest, adeA, adeB, adeC, adeI, adeJ, adeK, znuA, znuB, znuC, ttg2A, ttg2B and ttg2C. For each gene, the logFC of the 10 biocides (AgNO3, BZK, CTAB, CHL, TRC, CRL, PVPi, HClO, GLU and EtOH are listed accordingly. Additionally, the family (transporter family) for each gene is also listed. Genes adeA, adeB and adeC are assigned to the adeABC family. Genes adeI, adeJ and adeK are assigned to the adeIJK family. Genes znuA, znuB and znuC are assigned to the znuABC family. Genes ttg2A, ttg2B and ttg2C are assigned to the ttg2ABC family.
