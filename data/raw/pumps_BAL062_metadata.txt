pumps_BAL062.csv is an alteration of the tps.csv file. It retains the gene_name, family, biocide and logFC information of the 12 genes listed in tps.csv, but the term "family" is replaced by "group", short for "gene group". Additionally, this file also includes information on four additional genes. These genes are the Ttg2ABC-associated gene, mlcA, the ZnuABC-associated gene, zur, and the hypothetical proteins BAL062_00181 and BAL062_10982. Each of these new genes are associated with their own unique gene group, with the title of the group identical to the gene name. 
This dataset was used to conduct the Hypothesis 2 ANOVA, via the R script newgenes_anova.R. It was also used to construct Figure 5. and Figure 6 in R Studio via the R script, newgenes_barplots.R
